# Instrucciones de generación de las seis ilustraciones

Herramienta: generación de imágenes integrada.

## Capítulo 7

Use case: illustration-story. Create a single landscape chapter-opening illustration for the Spanish satirical novel Cuanto mejor, peor, chapter 7 El informe que nadie pidió. Black and white pen-and-ink editorial book illustration, fine crosshatching, softly feathered white vignette edges, classic humorous Spanish literary drawing. A local newspaper investigation represented by an anonymous journalist's hands opening an unmarked envelope on a modest newsroom desk: seventeen report sheets, a market building floor plan, a calendar date circled, a magnifying glass and a cold coffee. No visible faces, no invented portraits. The tension is quiet scrutiny rather than a scandalous crowd. Clear readable composition at 55 mm width, white paper background, no frame, no color, no chapter title or text or watermark. This is an entirely new scene; avoid town square benches, politicians at bar tables, elevators and piles of caricatured heads. Deliver one illustration only.
